## Contract and failure strategy

- Hermes Schema 2.0 is the sole producer and monetary source of truth.
- The BFF is a typed consumer/projection only. It does not persist, calculate,
  repair, backfill or refresh a Cost Estimate Snapshot.
- Schema 2.0 and every plan-level `cost_estimate` are required. Invalid required
  fields, enums, CNY ranges, category order, coupling, completeness or visible
  total reconciliation fail closed as a non-retryable Hermes protocol error.
- Unknown Hermes fields are ignored during typed parsing and cannot be emitted
  by the BFF response model. Snapshot identity, evidence, provider payloads,
  fen arithmetic and diagnostics are not public model fields.
- Existing ownership lookup and the local `result_record_id` plus `job_id`
  binding run before the Hermes result request. The returned `result_id` must
  still equal the requested record ID.
- Telemetry records only `result_schema_version` and the worst plan-level
  `cost_estimate_completeness`; it records no amount, price basis evidence,
  provider payload or user input.
